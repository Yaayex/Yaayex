## Selected projects

### 🧩 [goflow-engine](https://github.com/Yaayex/goflow-engine)

A high-performance, zero-dependency visual node-based programming engine written in Go.

Designed around speed, modularity and composable execution flows.

`Go` `Systems Design` `Visual Programming` `Zero Dependencies`

---

### 💸 [FlowMoney](https://github.com/Yaayex/FlowMoney)

A minimalistic but powerful end-to-end web application for managing financial flows.

Built with a focus on clarity, usability and a clean product experience.

`Web Application` `End-to-End` `Product Engineering`

---

### 🛰️ [WarpRoom](https://github.com/Yaayex/WarpRoom)

An ephemeral P2P space for instantly sharing text and files.

No unnecessary friction — open a room, share the data and let it disappear when it is no longer needed.

`P2P` `File Sharing` `Real-Time Systems`

---

### 🎧 [Resonance](https://github.com/Yaayex/Resonance)

An audio player designed as a free alternative to platforms such as Yandex Music and Spotify.

Focused on a more personal, lightweight and flexible listening experience.

`Audio` `Desktop Application` `Electron`

---

### 🔁 [Synchro](https://github.com/Yaayex/Synchro)

A platform that turns content consumption into a shared interactive experience.

Built around synchronization, presence and the feeling of being together online.

`Synchronization` `Social Experience` `Web`

---

### 🧱 [IronForge](https://github.com/Yaayex/IronForge)

A high-performance analytics backend for strength training.

Designed for processing workout data and turning raw numbers into useful insights.

`Go` `Analytics` `Backend Engineering`

---

### ⚙️ [TorqueCore](https://github.com/Yaayex/TorqueCore)

A project created in late 2025 to practice mathematical and computational concepts.

An experimental playground for algorithms, calculations and engineering ideas.

`Algorithms` `Mathematics` `Experimentation`

---

### 🔐 [sha256plus1](https://github.com/Yaayex/sha256plus1)

An extended SHA-256 experiment with an additional bit-oriented layer for UTF-8 compatibility.

A low-level exploration of hashing, encoding and data representation.

`Cryptography` `Hashing` `UTF-8`
